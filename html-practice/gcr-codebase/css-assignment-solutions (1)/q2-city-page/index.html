Kyoto

Overview

Kyoto was Japan's capital for over a thousand years, and it still feels like the country's spiritual heart — a city of quiet temples, wooden machiya houses, and narrow stone lanes.

Culture & Tradition

Home to over a thousand Buddhist temples and Shinto shrines, Kyoto preserves geisha districts, tea ceremonies, and centuries-old festivals that continue every year.

Food

From delicate kaiseki multi-course dinners to street-side matcha sweets, Kyoto's food scene reflects the same attention to detail as its gardens.

Planning a Visit

Spring cherry blossoms and autumn foliage are the most popular seasons, though a quiet winter morning at a temple has its own kind of magic.
